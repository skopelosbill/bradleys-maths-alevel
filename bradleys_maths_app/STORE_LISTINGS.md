WHY BRADLEY'S MATHS IS DIFFERENT

Most revision apps give you brief bullet points or jump straight to the final answer. We believe that true mathematical mastery comes from understanding every single step in between.

• No Skipped Steps: Every single question features a complete, full model answer that walks you through algebraic manipulations, calculus derivations, and geometric justifications line-by-line.
• The "Paper First" Mandate: Mathematics is learned with a pen and paper. The app actively prompts you to work out the full solution independently before revealing options.
• Diagnostic Misconception Feedback: Our multiple-choice distractors aren't random guesses. They are Plausibly Incorrect (PI) options designed to detect common student slips (such as sign errors, chain rule omissions, or incorrect limits). If you make a slip, the app pinpoints exactly what went wrong and gives you a second chance.
• The Head Teacher’s Eye (Bradley’s Insight): Benefit from decades of teaching wisdom. Every question includes a targeted insight block:
  - Deeper: Explores the underlying mathematical theory.
  - Pro-Tip: Fast, elegant exam techniques and time-saving shortcuts.
  - Caution: Red flags highlighting frequent examiner traps and classic student pitfalls.
