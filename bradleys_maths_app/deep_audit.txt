  [question_text]: The diagram shows a sketch of the curve $y = e^{ -x }$, where $x$ is measured in radians.<br>(a) Use the diagram to explain why the equation $$e^{ -x } - 1.5x - 0.8 = 0$$ has exactly one real root.<br>(b) Let the root be $\varepsilon$. Given that $\varepsilon$ is small, use the small-angle approximation for $e^{ -x }$ to estimate the value of $\varepsilon$ to 3 decimal places.
  [step_1]: From the diagram, $y = e^{ -x }$ decreases from 1 as x increases, while the line $y = 1.5x + 0.8$ increases.
  [step_2]: The two graphs cross once near the origin and do not meet again because the exponential decays while the line grows without bound.
  [step_3]: Using the small-angle approximation $e^{ -x } \approx 1 - x$, substitute into the equation: $$1 - x - 1.5x - 0.8 = 0.$$
  [step_4]: Simplifying gives \begin{aligned}0.2 - 2.5x &= 0 \cr x &= 0.08.\end{aligned}
  [step_5]: Final Answer: $$\varepsilon \approx 0.080.$$ <img src='images/Pure_PNGs/004009_ans.png' style='width:100%; max-width:400px; margin: 15px auto; display:block; border: 1px solid #ccc;'>
