Privacy Policy

App: AS and A Level Revision (uk.co.bradleysmaths.alevel)
Developer: Bradley's Maths
Last Updated: 2026

1. Overview

Bradley's Maths respects your privacy. The AS and A Level Revision app is built to be 100% offline-first, ad-free, and distraction-free. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The app does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers, usage analytics, or any other personal information. The app contains zero advertisements and zero third-party tracking SDKs.

3. Local On-Device Storage & Backup

All 1,400 questions are stored locally inside the app. Your study history, question attempts, Honour Wall progress, and preferences are saved strictly on your own device.

If you choose to use the optional Backup & Restore feature, your progress data is copied to or pasted from your device's clipboard solely under your own control. Bradley's Maths never transmits or receives this data on any server.

4. In-App Purchases

The optional one-off lifetime unlock is processed securely through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Bradley's Maths never sees, collects, or stores your payment card, bank, or billing information.

5. Children's Privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from any user, no personal data is collected from children or students of any age.
